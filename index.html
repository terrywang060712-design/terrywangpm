What to Track
Tracker

Simplify Jobs

Aggregates PM and APM internship listings in one place and autofills applications. Best way to apply to 30+ roles without burning hours on forms.

List

GitHub Internship List

Community-maintained list updated daily. Often the fastest way to see new postings before they surface on LinkedIn or company sites.

Compensation

Levels.fyi — Internships

Real intern pay data by company and role. Know the numbers before you compare or negotiate offers.

Networking

Cold LinkedIn Outreach

Message PMs and EPMs at target companies. Keep it to 3 sentences. A referral at a big tech company meaningfully changes your odds — especially from a non-target.

Interview Prep
What to Study

No filler. The resources below are the ones that actually move the needle for PM and APM interviews.

Books
Book

Cracking the PM Interview

Gayle McDowell & Jackie Bavaro. Covers every PM question type with clear structured approaches. Start here before anything else.

Book

Decode and Conquer

Lewis C. Lin. Tight, practical frameworks for design, estimation, and strategy questions. Best for structuring answers quickly under pressure.

Practice
Platform

Exponent

Best PM prep platform. Video lessons, real question bank, mock interviews with PMs. Subscribe for one month before your interview window.

Free Resource

IGotAnOffer — PM Blog

Free, detailed breakdowns of PM interviews at top companies with worked sample answers. One of the best free resources out there.

YouTube

Exponent on YouTube

Free mock PM interviews with real feedback. Watch 5–8 before your first interview to hear what a strong answer sounds like.

Newsletter

Lenny's Newsletter

Builds genuine product intuition over time. Gives you real things to reference when asked "what products excite you?"

Timeline
Recruiting Calendar

When to do what, for summer 2027 PM and EPM internships.

Aug – Sep 2026
Applications open

Apply immediately

  • Meta RPM, Microsoft, and Amazon open as early as August — apply day one
  • Finalize your resume and get feedback before this window
  • Set up LinkedIn job alerts and bookmark the GitHub internship list
Sep – Oct 2026
Prime networking window

Network for referrals

  • Send 3–5 LinkedIn cold messages per week to PMs/EPMs at target companies
  • Ask specifically for referrals — at non-targets, this changes your odds
  • Google APM opens mid-October and closes in ~2 weeks — be ready
Oct – Nov 2026
Interview prep

Prepare seriously

  • Read Cracking the PM Interview start to finish
  • Practice 10+ product design questions out loud using CIRCLES
  • Write and rehearse 6 STAR behavioral stories until they're cold
  • Do 3–4 mock interviews on Exponent or with a peer
Nov – Jan 2027
Interviews & offers

Interview season peaks

  • Most big tech PM/EPM interviews happen in this window
  • Keep applying — rolling admissions means late apps still get reviewed
  • Check Levels.fyi before comparing or negotiating offers
Jan – Mar 2027
Late season

Mid-size and startup hiring

  • Stripe, Snowflake, Palantir, and smaller companies hire on this later cycle
  • Some big tech roles reopen if headcount remains — re-apply where you can
  • If you have an offer, understand exploding deadlines before deciding
Advice
What Actually Worked

From getting an EPM role at Apple as a sophomore from a non-target school.

Referrals matter more than your school name. At a non-target, a referral from someone internal is often the difference between your resume getting seen or not. Treat networking as part of your application process, not optional.
Apply within 24 hours. PM and EPM cohorts are small. Applications that come in within the first day of a posting get meaningfully more attention than those submitted a week later.
Structure before you speak. In interviews, take 20–30 seconds to think before answering. Interviewers reward clear thinking over speed — silence is fine.
Anchor every decision to a user need. "I'd build X because users struggle with Y" is a PM answer. "I'd build X because it's cool" is not.
Practice out loud, not in your head. Answers feel very different when you say them. Record yourself once a week — you'll catch filler words and weak logic fast.
Don't overlook EPM and APM tracks. Engineering Program Manager and Associate PM roles are real paths into product. They're often more accessible as a sophomore and lead to the same career trajectory.

If you're a sophomore or junior at a non-target school trying to break into PM or EPM roles and feeling overwhelmed by the process — this is for you. The recruiting system isn't impossible to navigate. It just takes knowing where to look and moving fast when it counts.
