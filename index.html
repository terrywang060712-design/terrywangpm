Essential Frameworks

Reference
CIRCLES
Comprehend → Identify users → Report needs → Cut through → List solutions → Evaluate → Summarize
Product design questions — designing or improving a product.
RICE
Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort
Prioritization questions — comparing features or roadmap items.
AARRR
Acquisition → Activation → Retention → Referral → Revenue
Metrics / analytics questions — diagnosing a drop or defining success.
STAR
Situation → Task → Action → Result
Behavioral questions — tell me about a time you…
Jobs-to-be-Done
"When I ___, I want to ___, so I can ___."
User needs — reframe features around user motivations, not demographics.
North Star + Guardrails
One primary metric that captures value, protected by counter-metrics.
Success metrics — "how would you measure success for X?"

6-Week Study Plan

Timeline
Week 1
Foundation
  • Read Cracking the PM Interview (Part 1–2)
  • Watch 5 Exponent mock interviews on YouTube
  • Learn CIRCLES, STAR, RICE frameworks cold
Week 2
Product Design Questions
  • Practice 10 product design questions using CIRCLES
  • Read Inspired (Cagan) — key chapters on discovery and delivery
  • Record yourself answering 3 questions out loud
Week 3
Metrics & Analytical Thinking
  • Master AARRR and North Star metric frameworks
  • Practice 8 "diagnose a metric drop" questions
  • Study SQL basics if needed (Mode Analytics tutorial)
Week 4
Estimation & Strategy
  • Practice 8 market sizing / estimation questions
  • Read Decode and Conquer — strategy chapter
  • Prepare 2–3 strong opinions on products you use daily
Week 5
Behavioral & Company-Specific Prep
  • Write out 8 STAR stories covering leadership, conflict, data-driven decisions, failure
  • Study target company on Product Alliance
  • Read 4–5 Lenny's Newsletter posts relevant to target company's domain
Week 6
Mock Interviews & Polish
  • Do 4 full mock interviews (Exponent platform or with a peer)
  • Review recordings — cut filler words and tighten structure
  • Prepare 3 sharp questions to ask your interviewers

What Actually Works

Advice
Structure before you speak. Take 30 seconds to silently map your answer. Interviewers reward clear thinking far more than speed.
Anchor every product decision to a user need. "I would add X because users struggle with Y" beats "I would add X because it's cool."
Say the number. Estimation questions are about structured reasoning — always arrive at a final number, even if you caveat it.
Mock out loud, not in your head. Saying an answer feels completely different from thinking it. Record yourself at least once a week.
Have strong opinions on 2–3 products. "What product do you love and how would you improve it?" is the easiest question to nail if you've prepared it.
Know your stories cold. You should be able to deliver each STAR story in under 2 minutes from memory, not a script.
